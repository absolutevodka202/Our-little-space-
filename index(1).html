About Us

A little piece of our story, exactly the way I remember it.

DECEMBER 2025
♡

The Most Random Beginning

We met completely by coincidence on AirTalk. Neither of us expected anything serious—we thought it would just be time pass. But somehow we connected so easily, almost like we'd known each other before. I still remember your first picture: the green shirt, messy hair, shades, cute dimples and those pretty lips. And yes, I was Brinda at first 😭. Three days later I finally told you, “I'm Krutika.”

JANUARY 3, 2026
♡

The First Almost-Goodbye

We talked almost constantly until I got scared of getting too attached. I tried to disappear around Christmas, thinking leaving early would save me from getting hurt. But you still wished me on Christmas and New Year, and somehow I came back on January 3. After that, we just couldn't really stay away from each other.

FEBRUARY 2026
♡

Somewhere Between All Those Calls

After my birthday trip and all the missing each other, we became even closer. There were endless calls, silly conversations and our stupid little “Spidey thingy.” On February 9 you told me you loved me, and on February 11 you finally told me everything you felt. I was scared and overwhelmed, but you gave me time and never forced me. Somewhere in all of that, I finally realized that I loved you too. On February 14, we officially became us.

APRIL 2026
♡

When Everything Got Scary

Then my parents found our chats, and suddenly our little private world didn't feel private anymore. There were tears, fear and moments when I thought we had no choice but to end everything. We had to face things we hadn't been completely honest about, including me finally telling you my real age. Still, neither of us really wanted to say goodbye. So after everything, we chose to stay together till we could.

MAY 2026
♡

The Almost Goodbye

May hurt in a different way. There was a point when you decided we should slowly stop talking and eventually let each other go. I didn't know how to suddenly make my first love become a memory. I tried to accept that maybe I had to learn how to live without you, but somehow, after all the fear and hurt, we kept finding our way back to each other.

NOW
♡

The Little Things

Some of my happiest moments aren't big at all. It's being on call while you work or binge-watch something and I study. It's your little kisses over the phone, your teasing and nicknames, the way you notice even a tiny change in my mood, tell me to study, and sometimes fall asleep on call with me. Those ordinary things mean so much to me simply because they're ours.

“We were just two strangers who thought this would be nothing, and somehow, it became us.”

I made this little space because I don't want to forget the real parts of our story—the first conversation, the green shirt, Brinda becoming Krutika, January 3, February 14, the tears in April, the almost-goodbye in May, and everything that came after. If we ever forget how we got here, we can come back here and remember.
